## Sub-features

Contents navigation, first-chart tutorial and its approval, chart, sharing, restore and source-download images.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the public help contents. Choose **Codex로 첫 차트 만들기**, read the approval section and scroll to the revision 2 result.

## Browser evidence

Start at the contents in the requested browser, click the tutorial link, decode every inline image and compare the image bytes to the release checkout. Record screenshots for results and video for the journey. The optional automated recorder is under [Helpers](../SKILL.md#helpers). Restart the VitePress preview process after rebuilding so it serves the new assets.

## Gotchas

The tutorial illustrates two AI hosts. Viewing its illustrations proves guide delivery. A live host login and chart edit require separate evidence.
